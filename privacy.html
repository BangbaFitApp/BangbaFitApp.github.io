Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

BangBa Fit ("we", "our", or "us") values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the BangBa Fit mobile application.

Information We Collect

  • Name (if provided)
  • Email address
  • Authentication information through Apple or Google Sign-In
  • Workout history
  • Exercise progress
  • XP points and achievements
  • Challenge submissions
  • User-generated videos uploaded within the app

How We Use Your Information

  • Provide workout tracking
  • Save your progress
  • Display leaderboards
  • Calculate XP rewards
  • Improve the app experience
  • Provide customer support

Third-Party Services

BangBa Fit uses Firebase services provided by Google for authentication, database storage, analytics, and secure cloud services.

Subscriptions

Premium subscriptions are processed securely through Apple App Store or Google Play. BangBa Fit never stores your payment information.

Data Security

We use industry-standard security practices to protect your information. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Your Rights

You may request deletion of your account and associated personal information by contacting us.

Children

BangBa Fit is not intended for children under 13 years of age.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

Contact

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the Contact Us section inside the BangBa Fit app.
