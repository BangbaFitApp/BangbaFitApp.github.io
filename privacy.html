1. Introduction & Scope

Welcome to BangBa Fit (“BangBa Fit,” “we,” “our,” or “us”). This Privacy Policy explains how BangBa Fit collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects information when you use the BangBa Fit mobile application, related websites, and services that link to this Privacy Policy (the “Services”). It also explains choices and rights that may be available to you.

By using BangBa Fit, you acknowledge the data practices described here. Where applicable law requires consent for particular processing, BangBa Fit will request it separately. This policy should be read with our Terms of Use.

2. Eligibility & Children’s Privacy

BangBa Fit is intended for users age 13 or older, subject to any higher minimum age required by applicable law. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Users below the age of majority should use BangBa Fit with permission or supervision required by applicable law. If we learn that impermissible information was collected from a child under 13, we will take reasonable steps to delete it and, where appropriate, restrict or terminate the associated account.

Parents or guardians may contact bangbafit@outlook.com. U.S. information is available from the FTC COPPA resource.

3. Information We Collect

Account & Profile

We may process your name or display name, email, profile photo if provided, account identifier, authentication information received through supported sign-in methods, preferences, settings, and information you voluntarily add. BangBa Fit does not need your Apple or Google password when those providers authenticate you.

Fitness & Workout

We may process workouts, exercise history, repetitions, weight or resistance values you enter, duration, saved statistics, plans, schedules, completion status, progress, XP, achievements, rankings, streaks, and challenge results. Unless expressly stated otherwise, these are not medical records or medical-grade measurements.

User Content

We may process videos, photos, challenge submissions and scores, captions, comments, reports, and information used to review or verify results. Content intentionally published to public or community features may be visible to other users.

Permissions, Transactions & Technical Data

Depending on features you use, BangBa Fit may process camera, microphone, or photo/media information you intentionally provide; subscription status, product identifiers, transaction identifiers, renewal/expiration and restore-purchase information; and limited device, operating-system, app-version, diagnostic, network, security, authentication, or connection information.

Information Not Intentionally Requested

Unless a future feature clearly requests it and disclosures are updated, BangBa Fit does not intentionally request government identification numbers, banking credentials, full payment-card information, precise location solely for ordinary workout functionality, medical diagnoses, or medical records.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use information to create and authenticate accounts; provide workouts, plans, recovery content, progress, subscriptions, challenges, leaderboards, XP and rewards; moderate community features; answer support and privacy requests; protect accounts and prevent fraud; diagnose and improve the Services; and comply with law or valid legal process.

Where reasonably possible, aggregated or de-identified information may be used for analysis and improvement.

BangBa Fit does not sell users’ personal information for monetary payment.

5. How We Share Information & Service Providers

BangBa Fit shares information only as reasonably necessary to operate, secure, maintain, and improve the Services; comply with law; protect users; or as otherwise described here.

Firebase, Apple & Google

BangBa Fit may use Firebase and related Google services for authentication, databases, storage, security, technical operations, and diagnostics or analytics where enabled. BangBa Fit may interact with Apple and Google for authentication, app distribution, subscriptions, and supported platform functionality.

See Firebase Privacy and Security, Apple Privacy, and Google Privacy.

Other Users & Providers

Information you intentionally publish through community features may be visible to other users. Trusted providers may process information for cloud hosting, authentication, security, support, diagnostics, analytics where enabled, or subscription verification.

Information may also be disclosed for valid legal process, safety, fraud/security investigations, legal claims, a permitted business transfer, or at your direction or valid consent.

BangBa Fit does not currently operate an advertising-supported business model.

6. Subscriptions & Payment Information

Apple or Google processes payments for subscriptions purchased through its respective store. BangBa Fit does not receive or store your complete credit/debit-card information from those stores.

We may process limited subscription information to determine premium access, restore purchases, verify status, troubleshoot access, prevent fraud, and maintain appropriate records.

Deleting your BangBa Fit account does not necessarily cancel an active App Store or Google Play subscription. Manage cancellation separately through the platform where purchased.

7. Camera, Microphone, Photos & Device Permissions

BangBa Fit requests device permissions when needed for a feature. Granting permission does not mean continuous access.

Camera access may support workout or challenge recording. Microphone access may capture audio for a recording you intentionally initiate. Photo/media access may let you select supported media, save recordings, choose a profile image, or share content.

BangBa Fit does not intentionally use the microphone for unrelated background listening or surveillance. You can manage permissions through device settings. Permission granted for one purpose is not treated as unrestricted permission for materially unrelated purposes.

8. User Content, Challenge Videos & Public Information

Depending on the feature, your display name, profile image, published challenge videos, scores, leaderboard positions, displayed XP or achievements, comments, and other contributions may be visible to other users.

Challenge content may be processed to store/display submissions, associate them with accounts, record scores, verify performance, determine rankings/rewards, investigate reports, and enforce rules. Moderation may use user reports, manual review, automated systems, or technical indicators where implemented.

Do not publish sensitive information you do not want others to see. You are responsible for permissions required to record or publish other identifiable people.

Where supported, content may be deleted or removal requested, although limited copies may temporarily remain for backups, security, fraud investigations, disputes, enforcement, or legal obligations. BangBa Fit cannot guarantee another user has not independently preserved information that was made visible to them.

BangBa Fit will not use identifiable User Content in external advertising solely because you published it; additional permission will be obtained where legally required.

9. Data Retention & Account Deletion

BangBa Fit retains personal information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described here, legal obligations, disputes, security, fraud prevention, and enforcement.

Where supported, users may initiate account deletion in the app or contact bangbafit@outlook.com. Reasonable verification may be required.

Deletion may remove or de-identify profile information, workout history, progress, plans, XP, achievements, challenge history/videos, leaderboard information, unredeemed rewards, and other account-specific information, as applicable.

Limited information may be retained for legal, tax, accounting, regulatory, security, fraud-prevention, dispute, enforcement, transaction-record, or legal-claim purposes. Deleted information may remain temporarily in secure backups. Aggregated or de-identified information may be retained where permitted by law.

Account deletion does not necessarily cancel an active Apple App Store or Google Play subscription.

10. Data Security

BangBa Fit takes reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect personal information. Measures may include authentication controls, access restrictions, secure backend communications, database/storage security rules, and suspicious-activity protections.

If a security incident occurs, BangBa Fit may investigate, mitigate harm, secure systems, work with providers, preserve necessary evidence, and notify users or authorities where required by law.

Report suspected unauthorized account access to bangbafit@outlook.com.

No application, database, cloud service, or internet transmission method can guarantee 100% security.

11. Your Privacy Rights & Choices

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, obtain information about processing, receive portable data, object to or restrict processing, withdraw consent where applicable, appeal certain decisions, or complain to an appropriate privacy authority.

You can manage supported device permissions and push notifications through device settings. Where functionality exists, you may also edit or remove certain information directly in BangBa Fit.

To exercise an applicable privacy right, contact bangbafit@outlook.com. We may request reasonable verification and will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law. Where prohibited by law, BangBa Fit will not unlawfully discriminate against you for exercising an applicable privacy right.

12. International Users & Cross-Border Data Processing

BangBa Fit is operated from the United States and may use providers whose systems, personnel, or infrastructure are located in the United States or other countries. Information may therefore be processed outside your country of residence.

Where applicable data-protection law requires specific safeguards for international transfers, BangBa Fit will take reasonable steps to use an appropriate lawful transfer mechanism or other required safeguard.

Users in jurisdictions with comprehensive privacy laws may have additional rights. Nothing in this policy is intended to eliminate rights that cannot lawfully be waived.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

BangBa Fit may update this policy to reflect changes in Services, technologies, data practices, law, security, or operational needs. The “Last Updated” date will be revised, and material changes may receive additional notice where appropriate or legally required.

An updated policy does not automatically authorize materially different use of previously collected information where applicable law requires additional consent. BangBa Fit will request such consent where required.

14. Contact Us

For questions, concerns, complaints, account requests, or privacy-right requests, contact:

Email: bangbafit@outlook.com

We may request reasonable verification for sensitive requests and will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law.

Our Privacy Commitment

BangBa Fit believes that building a strong fitness community also requires earning and maintaining users’ trust. We are committed to transparency, legitimate use of information, reasonable safeguards, and meaningful user control.

Your progress belongs to you. Your privacy deserves respect.
