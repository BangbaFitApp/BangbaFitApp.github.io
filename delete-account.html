Account & Data Deletion

Last Updated August 21, 2026
BangBa Fit allows users to request deletion of their account and associated personal information.

You may delete your account from the BangBa Fit app or contact us if you cannot access the in-app deletion option.

How to Delete Your Account

Option 1 — From the BangBa Fit App

  1. Open BangBa Fit.
  2. Go to your Profile screen.
  3. Select Delete Account.
  4. Review the deletion information.
  5. Confirm your deletion request.
  6. BangBa Fit will begin processing deletion of your account and associated data.

Option 2 — Request Deletion by Email

If you cannot access your account or the in-app deletion feature, email:

bangbafit@outlook.com

We recommend using the subject line Account Deletion Request.

For security, BangBa Fit may request reasonable information to verify that you are authorized to request deletion of the account.

What May Be Deleted

Depending on the information associated with your account, deletion may include:

  • Account and profile information.
  • Workout history and saved progress.
  • Workout plans.
  • Exercise statistics.
  • XP and achievements.
  • Challenge submissions and challenge history.
  • Challenge videos and other User Content.
  • Leaderboard-related account information.
  • Unredeemed rewards.
  • Other personal information associated with the account.

Information We May Need to Retain

Limited information may be retained where reasonably necessary or legally required for purposes such as:

  • Fraud and abuse prevention.
  • Security and system integrity.
  • Resolving disputes.
  • Enforcing applicable terms or rules.
  • Transaction records.
  • Tax, accounting, regulatory, or legal requirements.
  • Establishing, exercising, or defending legal claims.

Deleted information may also remain temporarily in secure backups until removed through normal backup-retention processes.

Important Subscription Notice

Deleting your BangBa Fit account does not automatically cancel an active Apple App Store or Google Play subscription.

You should separately cancel your subscription through the platform where it was purchased to prevent future renewal charges.

Need Help?

For questions about account or data deletion, contact:

bangbafit@outlook.com

You can also review our Privacy Policy for more information about data retention, deletion, and privacy rights.
